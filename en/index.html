CASE 01 · ABB ROBOT

Intermittent robot stop under load

Problem: The robot cell stopped irregularly during cycle. Analysis: I/O status, sequence conditions and RAPID logic were traced during operation. Result: The fault area was isolated and a controlled restart could be verified.

CASE 02 · VISION

Image set timeout

Problem: The vision flow generated sporadic timeout alarms. Analysis: Camera trigger, cycle sequence and wait states were compared. Result: The risk area for false timeout events was identified and verification points were defined.

Intermittent communication fault

Problem: Temporary communication loss between PLC, drives and field devices. Analysis: Diagnostic buffer, network status and signal loss were reviewed. Result: The fault chain was narrowed for targeted action and follow-up.
