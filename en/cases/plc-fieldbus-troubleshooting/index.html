PLC and fieldbus: intermittent communication fault

An anonymised case on narrowing down communication loss between a PLC, drive system and field devices using diagnostics.

An anonymised case. Client names and sensitive production details are omitted. Observations, diagnosis and verified outcomes are kept separate.

Status: Fault chain narrowed down for targeted action and follow-up

Starting point

Temporary communication loss occurred between the PLC, drive system and field devices. A communication alarm can be part of a larger fault chain, so the visible alarm needs comparison with network status and the signals that became unavailable.

My focus

The diagnostic buffer, network status and signal loss were reviewed to narrow down the sequence of events. The focus was systematic troubleshooting and signal verification, not developing a new PLC program or attributing the fault to a single component without evidence.

Diagnostic approach

Follow communication and relevant signals through the system. Compare when a fault is logged with when a device or signal becomes unavailable. This helps distinguish a possible cause from consequential alarms, but correlation in time is not, by itself, final proof of the root cause.

Reported outcome

The fault chain was narrowed down for targeted action and follow-up. The case does not report a verified defective component, a confirmed cable replacement or a finally verified resolution. No percentage for availability or downtime reduction is stated.

Further verification

The next step should follow the collected diagnostics and the facility’s approved procedures. A temporarily restored connection requires follow-up under relevant operating conditions before sustained stability can be assessed. Changes and test results should be recorded so that follow-up can be compared with the original symptoms.

Key learning

A clearly isolated fault area is a useful result even when the final corrective action remains open. Reporting uncertainty and the next verification step makes the handover more useful than a general statement that communication is working again.
